# better-riftdecks

A browser extension for cleaner browsing on [RiftDecks](https://riftdecks.com/), [MTGDecks](https://mtgdecks.net/), and [InkDecks](https://inkdecks.com/).

- Blocks common ads and trackers and removes empty ad spaces.
- Hides visual decklists while keeping the text list and stats.
- Restores Command-click and Ctrl-click on matching links in clickable rows.

The extension only runs on these sites and does not collect data. Its cleanup was built for RiftDecks, so results on the other sites may vary.

## Install

Download or clone this repository, then follow the steps for your browser.

**Chrome**

1. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode**.
2. Select **Load unpacked** and choose the `better-riftdecks` folder.

**Firefox 142 or later**

1. Open `about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox`.
2. Select **Load Temporary Add-on** and choose `manifest.json` in the `better-riftdecks` folder.

Firefox removes temporary add-ons when it exits. Load it again after restarting. Permanent installation requires a Mozilla-signed add-on.

After installing or updating the extension, reload any open pages on these sites. After updating its files, reload the extension from your browser's extensions page first.
